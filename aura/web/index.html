    Welcome to Aura

    Step 1 of 3

    Aura is a companion that runs entirely on this computer. She answers using a model you run yourself in LM Studio, and she works inside one protected folder.

    Her workspace is …. Everything she writes goes there, and everything she remembers stays on this machine. There is no account, no cloud service, and no API key.

    Aura needs LM Studio running with a model loaded. Start LM Studio, load a model, and turn on its local server — then check the connection here.

    You are ready. A few things worth knowing:

    • Ask her to build something. She creates and edits files in her workspace, and every change can be undone.
    • She cannot reach the rest of your computer until you grant a folder under Permissions. She can never grant it to herself.
    • Anything risky asks first — running a project, installing packages, or reaching the network.
    • If something misbehaves, use Export report under the action log; it describes the problem without including anything you said.
